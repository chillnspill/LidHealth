A little about us

Good care should feel within reach.

Many lid care products are expensive, and patients stop using them when the cost outweighs the benefit. LidHealth keeps prices low so a daily routine is easy to keep.

The spray is hypochlorous acid (HOCl), an antimicrobial that works best below pH 7.5 and is gentle enough for delicate eyelid skin. The oil blends tea tree, frankincense and lavender to clean, moisturise and soothe the lids.
