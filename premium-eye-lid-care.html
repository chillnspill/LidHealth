Directions

How to use each item

Lid & Lash Spray

HOCl is a natural disinfecting agent that kills bacteria, viruses and fungi.

Spray onto closed eye lids and leave to air dry. Use mornings and evenings, as well as after exposure to wind & dust. It will not burn the eyes.

Therapeutic Lid Oil

A combination of organic and essential oils that treat inflammation. This light oil absorbs easily and will not clog the pores. Effective against Demodex infestation.

Dispense a small amount onto a clean finger and apply over eye lids and lashes. Give special attention to lid margins. Best to use after using the Lid & Lash Spray. Use mornings and evenings.

Therapeutic Eye Mask

This mask is filled with flax seeds and dried lavender, to soothe and calm tired eyes and inflamed eye lids.

Heat in microwave for 20–30 seconds. Apply over eyes and leave for 10 minutes until the mask has completely cooled down. This will stimulate the Meibomian glands and help to treat dry eyes. Use daily for best results.
